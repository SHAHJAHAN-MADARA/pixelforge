Frequently Asked Questions
Are my images safe?

Yes. PixelForge processes everything in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device — no upload, no server, no storage.

Is there a file size limit?

There's no hard limit. Your browser can handle most images up to ~50 MB. For best performance, we recommend images under 20 MB.

What image formats are supported?

PixelForge works with JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG images. Output formats include JPEG, PNG, and WebP.

Can I batch process multiple images?

Currently each tool processes one image at a time. We're working on batch support for future updates.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Once the page loads, all processing happens locally. You can even use it without internet after the first load.
