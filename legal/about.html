About PixelForge

Free image tools for everyone

What is PixelForge?

PixelForge is a collection of free online image tools — compress, resize, crop, convert, rotate, apply filters, pick colors, generate favicons, and more. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API.

Privacy First

Your images are never uploaded to any server. All processing happens locally on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit your files. When you close the tab, everything is gone.

No Signup Required

There are no accounts, no paywalls, no usage limits, and no watermarks. Every tool is completely free to use, forever.

Open & Transparent

PixelForge uses standard web technologies — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No server-side processing, no hidden tracking, no third-party analytics.
